Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how pompelmi.app uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies. The site is operated by Tommaso Bertocchi (Italy). For privacy-related matters see the Privacy Policy.

1. What This Site Uses

pompelmi.app uses only essential technical storage: a single localStorage entry to remember that you have accepted this cookie policy. No HTTP cookies are set by this site itself.

Essential storage is strictly necessary for a basic site feature (the consent banner) and does not require opt-in consent under ePrivacy rules.

2. Essential localStorage Entry

Key Storage type Purpose Expires
pompelmi_cookie_consent localStorage Remembers that you dismissed the cookie consent banner so it does not reappear on subsequent visits. Until you clear browser storage

This entry is written only when you click Accept on the consent banner. It contains the value "1" and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Cookies

This site does not use any third-party form backends or tracking services. No third-party cookies are set on any page of pompelmi.app.

4. No Tracking or Analytics

This site does not use:

  • Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, or any other analytics service
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Browser fingerprinting scripts
  • Any third-party JavaScript except the Formspree form endpoint

5. Managing and Deleting Cookies

To remove the consent preference stored by this site, open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage → pompelmi.app, and delete the pompelmi_cookie_consent key. The consent banner will reappear on your next visit.

You can also instruct your browser to block or delete all cookies and localStorage data. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions:

  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge
